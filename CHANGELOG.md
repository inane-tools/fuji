# Changelog

All notable changes to Fuji are documented here. The format is based on
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and this project
adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-27

Kanagawa-oki.

### Added

- The library as a justified mosaic under month and day headers, with a zoom
  slider (and Ctrl+scroll), a timeline scrubber and a cached index that opens
  instantly and refreshes in the background.
- Memories, albums, shared albums, media types (Videos, Live Photos, Portrait,
  Panoramas, Slo-mo, Screenshots and more), Hidden and Recently Deleted.
- A viewer with zoom and pan, video playback with sound, a filmstrip, Live
  Photo motion and an info panel (camera, lens, exposure, location, the app
  that saved it).
- Search by date, type, favorites or file name as you type, and through a
  paired iPhone's on-device index.
- Copy, download originals, drag out, and upload by button, drop or paste.
  Favorite, and delete with a few seconds to undo. Changes from other devices
  show up within seconds.
- Sign-in with an Apple Account and device approval; tokens and keys kept in
  the system's credential store.
- Light and dark mode, Windows' accent color or Apple's blue, and a custom
  title bar.
- An updater: Fuji checks GitHub for new releases, verifies their signature,
  and installs them when you choose (Windows and AppImage). A Windows
  installer, a Linux AppImage and a Linux Flatpak.
