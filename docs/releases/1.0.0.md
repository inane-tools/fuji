Fuji 1.0.0 **Kanagawa-oki** is the first release: a native iCloud Photos client for Windows and Linux.

- **Library**: your whole library as a justified mosaic by month and day, with zoom and a timeline scrubber, opening instantly from a cached index.
- **Collections**: Memories, albums, shared albums, media types, Hidden and Recently Deleted.
- **Viewer**: full-size photos with zoom and pan, videos with sound, Live Photos, and an info panel with camera, lens, exposure and location.
- **Search** by date, type, favorites or file name, and, paired with your iPhone, by what its on-device index knows (“dog”, “beach”).
- **Copy, download, drag out and upload** by button, drop or paste. Favorite, and delete with undo.
- **Updates**: Fuji checks GitHub and shows a ring on your account picture when a new version is ready to install.
